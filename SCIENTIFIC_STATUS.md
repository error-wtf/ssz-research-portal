# Scientific status

Segmented Spacetime (SSZ) is a mathematically concrete and falsifiable strong-field research programme. It is not a complete or empirically confirmed fundamental theory of gravity.

The current public canonical scope is a static, spherical effective metric with a locked strong branch, a derivative-matched quintic C² transition on 1.8 ≤ r/r_s ≤ 2.2, and a weak branch. The strongest secured theoretical result is finite static-clock dilation at r=r_s within that declared geometry.

The diagonal continuation is not regular at the areal centre: A→1/4, R~3/(2r²), and K~9/(4r⁴). A physical global completion requires a new interior solution or a fully specified boundary geometry. Rotation, perturbations, waveforms and image-domain astrophysical inference remain incomplete.

Internal software results establish properties of encoded equations and recorded data pipelines. They are not empirical confirmation.
