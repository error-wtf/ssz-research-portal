# Symbol Glossary

`data/symbols.json` contains 22 curated symbols. The public glossary explains Ξ, D, A, φ, r, r_s, R, K, J, Φ, proper time, PPN parameters and units.

Notation rules:

- `r` is the areal radius unless explicitly stated otherwise.
- `x=r/r_s` is the preferred dimensionless plotting coordinate.
- `φ` is the golden ratio in the current strong branch.
- `D` and `Ξ` are dimensionless.
- `R` and `K` have dimensions length⁻² and length⁻⁴.
