Internal rejection conditions

Canonical mismatch

Wrong exponent, reversed \(r/r_s\), wrong branch inclusivity or a bridge that does not match value, slope and curvature rejects the claimed canonical implementation.

Mathematical inconsistency

Dimensional failure, broken metric inverse, wrong limit, discontinuous curvature or irreconcilable derivations reject the affected claim.

Irreproducibility

A headline number without frozen code, inputs, tolerances, environment and result hash is not admissible evidence.

Versioned forward predictions

Observational exclusion requires a complete comparison

Necessary ingredients

  • locked SSZ metric and branch;
  • observable-specific forward model;
  • measurement covariance and systematics;
  • declared nuisance parameters and priors;
  • valid alternative model;
  • complexity treatment;
  • predeclared exclusion statistic.

Decision wording

Use “this SSZ version is excluded for this observable and domain” when its predictive region misses the data under the frozen protocol. Do not claim that one exclusion automatically rules out every conceivable alternative interior or future theory.

Preregister before looking

Minimal falsification protocol

  1. Freeze the Claim-ID and metric commit

    No post-data branch or parameter changes under the same result label.

  2. Publish inputs and covariance

    Hash raw data, preprocessing and masks; state unavailable licensed inputs.

  3. Declare statistic and threshold

    Include complexity, nuisance and look-elsewhere handling.

  4. Run controls and alternatives

    Negative controls, injection recovery, ablation and valid competing models.

  5. Retain every outcome

    Publish exclusions, null results, failures and sensitivity to assumptions.
