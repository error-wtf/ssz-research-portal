Symbol index

A(r)

D²(r), the positive lapse-like metric function in the diagonal ansatz. A→1/4 at the formal centre.

Ξ(r)

Dimensionless segment-density field. Its operative form depends on the declared radial regime.

D(r)

Dimensionless time factor 1/(1+Ξ), relating dτ to dt for static observers.

φ

Golden ratio (1+√5)/2≈1.6180339887 used in the canonical strong branch.

r_s

Schwarzschild length scale 2GM/c².

R, K

Ricci scalar and Kretschmann scalar. In the current diagonal centre limit they diverge as r⁻² and r⁻⁴.

J, Φ

JIF cycle count and accumulated phase, related by J=Φ/(2π).

γ, β

PPN parameters. The declared SSZ weak-field completion uses γ=β=1.

z

Static redshift proxy z=1/D−1=Ξ within the declared clock mapping.

Glossary

Areal radius

The radius defined by a symmetry sphere’s area 4πr². Its zero is geometrically significant.

Canonical

The currently selected source or definition within the project hierarchy. A later P0 correction can supersede an older file labelled canonical.

Coordinate singularity

A chart failure removable by changing coordinates, unlike an invariant curvature divergence.

Empirical compatibility

Data do not reject a model within a defined analysis. It is weaker than unique confirmation.

Event horizon

A global causal boundary, definable only from the full spacetime—not merely a zero or finite value of one local coefficient.

Falsifiability

The existence of observations or mathematical results that could rule out a model.

Geodesic completeness

Whether all inextendible geodesics have complete affine/proper parameter ranges. Finite coefficients do not prove it.

JIF

An operational count of physical phase cycles, not a new absolute time unit.

Legacy

Historical material retained for development history but not used as current scientific authority.

Model lock

A frozen formula/parameter/method specification used to make downstream results reproducible.

P0 correction

A highest-priority scientific correction. Here it retracts complete singularity freedom and restores the correct interior scope.

PPN

Parameterized post-Newtonian formalism for systematic weak-field comparisons.

Proper time

The invariant time measured by a clock along a timelike worldline.

Regular centre

An areal centre with appropriate local geometry and finite curvature invariants—not merely finite metric components.

Software validation

Evidence that code satisfies specified tests. It is not direct evidence that nature selects the model.

Strong field

A regime where weak-field expansions are insufficient and full metric/geodesic dynamics matter.

Frequently asked questions

Is SSZ a complete theory of gravity?

No. It is a concrete effective research programme with geometry, calculations and tests, but lacks a completed fundamental action, global interior and several dynamical sectors.

Does SSZ remove the black-hole singularity?

It regularises the declared horizon time factor. The present canonical diagonal continuation still has divergent curvature at the areal centre. Complete singularity freedom is not established.

Does local light slow down?

No local observer measures a vacuum light speed other than c. Coordinate speeds can change with coordinates and metric coefficients.

Why use φ?

φ is structurally selected in the current branch. A deeper dynamical derivation of why nature should select it remains an open foundational question.

Is JIF just a period?

No. It is the number of accumulated cycles. A period is time per cycle; JIF is phase divided by 2π.

What would falsify SSZ?

Locked strong-field forward predictions that fail sufficiently precise data, weak-field regressions, or a mathematical inconsistency in any proposed dynamical completion.

Why are there multiple test totals?

Different reports count functions, parameterised cases, custom phases, repositories or historical result artefacts. A valid total must state its scope and command.

Can a passing fit prove SSZ?

No. Model flexibility, data selection, nuisance parameters, covariance and alternative models must be assessed independently.
