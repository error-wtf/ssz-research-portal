Cross-repository rules

Single scientific vocabulary

Ξ, D, r_s, branch domains and observable methods must mean the same thing across repositories. A local toy formula must carry a toy/legacy label.

Locks before downstream results

Branch, field and model locks should precede expensive data runs. Results must record the exact lock and commit they consumed.

No silent result promotion

A plot repository may visualise a result but does not raise its epistemic status. The underlying derivation and test provenance remain authoritative.

Dirty working trees

The inventory records local modification state without changing those repositories. Unrelated working changes were preserved.
