Superseded

“Finite curvature everywhere”

The current diagonal continuation has \(R\sim3/(2r^2)\) and \(K\sim9/(4r^4)\) at the areal centre. Passing transition tests cannot establish central regularity.

Overstatement

“Tests confirm the theory”

Tests confirm encoded assertions. Empirical preference additionally requires measured data, uncertainty, alternatives, preregistered criteria and independent replication.
