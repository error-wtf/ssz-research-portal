Solar Galactic Year: three models, one consistency check

The repository combines an approximately 230 Myr declared orbit with a point-mass Sgr A* velocity calculation and a weak SSZ clock correction. This module keeps those layers separate so that a fixed observed period is not mistaken for a Kepler prediction from the black hole alone.

Observed-kinematic model

\[T_{\rm kin}=\frac{2\pi R_0}{v_c}\]

This uses an adopted Galactocentric radius and total circular speed. It represents the Galaxy’s net gravitational potential only through the measured speed.

Point-mass countercheck

\[T_{\rm BH}=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R_0^3}{GM_{\rm SgrA*}}}\]

Sgr A* alone cannot reproduce the Solar circular speed at 8.3 kpc. The discrepancy exposes the missing extended Galactic mass distribution.

SSZ clock layer

\[\Xi=\frac{r_s}{2R_0},\quad D=(1+\Xi)^{-1},\quad T_{\rm clock}=T_{\rm kin}/D\]

At the Solar radius this correction is extremely small. It must not be used to repair the point-mass dynamical mismatch.

Repository consistency finding: the README’s roughly 247.8 km/s and the JSON’s roughly 1.492 km/s refer to incompatible dynamical assumptions. The module preserves both as an audit result and links to the source repository.
