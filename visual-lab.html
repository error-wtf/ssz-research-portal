P0 asymptotic diagnostic

Approaching the areal centre

On logarithmic axes, the canonical diagonal continuation has R∼3/(2r²) and K∼9/(4r⁴). The steeper K line exposes why finite A→1/4 does not imply central regularity.

Interpretation: these are stated leading asymptotics, not a numerical solution at r=0. Both invariants diverge as the radius tends to zero.
