Canonical weak branch

\[\Xi_{\rm weak}(r)=\frac{r_s}{2r}=\frac{GM}{rc^2}\]
\[D(r)=\frac{1}{1+\Xi(r)}\longrightarrow1\]

The operative weak branch begins beyond 2.2 rs. Its first-order expansion is D ≈ 1−U with U=GM/(rc²), giving the standard leading gravitational clock term and asymptotic flatness.

Metric expansion

\[A=D^2=1-2U+3U^2+\mathcal O(U^3)\]
\[B=D^{-2}=1+2U+U^2+\mathcal O(U^3)\]

The leading coefficients reproduce the reference weak-field structure. Higher-order terms, observable definitions and complete propagation/orbit equations determine whether a specific measurement can discriminate models.
