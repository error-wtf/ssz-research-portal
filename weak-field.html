Weak-field limits

At large radius the public SSZ profile approaches the Newtonian/PPN regime. This tab separates mathematical limiting behaviour from empirical discrimination.

Canonical weak branch

\[\Xi_{\mathrm{weak}}(r)=\frac{r_s}{2r},\qquad D=(1+\Xi)^{-1}\to1\]

With \(r_s=2GM/c^2\), the first-order clock term is \(D\approx1-GM/(rc^2)\). The branch is asymptotically flat within the declared static geometry.

Observable discipline

Clock comparisons, light propagation, perihelion motion and lensing require different complete forward models. Agreement with \(\beta=\gamma=1\) is compatibility with the weak-field reference, not a unique empirical victory for SSZ.
