Construct a quintic interior candidate for A(r) on 0≤r≤rm. Six conditions fix the polynomial: A(0), A′(0)=0, A″(0), and value/slope/curvature matching to the canonical exterior at rm. Passing these local checks is not a global solution.

Scope: this is a constrained metric-ansatz laboratory, not a solver for SSZ field equations. It can reject candidates that fail local conditions; it cannot certify matter consistency, stability, causal completeness or a physical global interior.
